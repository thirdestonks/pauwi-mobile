The Weight

One Juan, one bag, six screens. Everything here shares the same state: drop something on the Bag screen and the map's load, the step cost and the odds of running from a fight all change. Give the antibiotics to Lorna and they're gone from the bag. Eat the rice and you're 2 kg lighter. All of it is drawn with the new paintings.

1 · The map

The bag on the map fills up with what he carries. The subtitle says what this load costs.
  • Light ≤ 6 kg: a fresh step costs 2 Energy, run +10%.
  • Carrying ≤ 12: 3 Energy a fresh step, as today.
  • Loaded ≤ 16: 5 Energy fresh, 1 on cleared ground, dash 2 hexes, run −15%, louder in houses.
  • Overloaded ≤ 20: 8 Energy fresh, 2 cleared, no dash, run −30%, twice as loud. Past 20 nothing goes in.

2 · The bag

Kilos under every item. Tap one to drop it and watch the other screens.
  • Ten slots stay (the grid). Weight is the real limit.
  • What he wears and holds counts: it's all on his body.
  • Water is heavy, medicine is light. That's why everyone wants medicine.

3 · Using it in a fight

Gauze takes two turns, on his own hands. Bitten mid-wrap and it's ruined.
JUAN

  • Gauze: 2 turns, +25 Health. Pills, water, a can: 1 turn each. Rice can't be eaten in a fight.
  • Run odds follow the load: it's on this screen's button.
  • Out on the map, wrapping a wound costs 1 move: he sits down to do it.

4 · Someone asks

Lorna wants medicine for her son. What you give is gone. What you refuse comes back.
  • Eight people, each met once a run (the roster is below).
  • Saying no is never free: Dread now, and something days later.

5 · Mang Isko

The knife sharpener, nights only. Play his upgrade mini-games →
  • Fix it: back to full swings, ½ a point per missing swing.
  • Sharpen / Nail it: +1 damage for 12 swings, 6 points.
  • Sell him steel: he pays 0.8 for weapons, and it comes off your back.

6 · Eating at home

Tap the counter. The meal sits there and steams; then it's gone, and so is its weight.
  • Eating is shown, not listed: the bowl appears on Nida's counter.
  • Rice weighs 2 kg, so carrying it home to cook is a real choice.
