His stall, playable. Pick a weapon, pick an upgrade, bring the materials, then either work it with him now (a mini-game: how well you do sets the quality) or leave it overnight (always Good, but the weapon is gone until tomorrow night). Melee only: guns are another trader, later. Back to The Weight.

Grades

  • ★ Perfect: the upgrade plus a bonus (+2 swings, or +5% crits on an edge).
  • Good: the upgrade as written. Overnight jobs are always Good.
  • ✗ Botched: no upgrade, the material is gone, he keeps half the points. A burnt edge also costs 2 swings.

Rules

  • Three upgrades per weapon. Each one costs more on a weapon that already has work on it (×1, ×1.5, ×2).
  • Every upgrade has a minus (weight, swings, sticking, two hands). Check the stat block before you commit.
  • His respect grows with jobs done: at 3 he puts his own bolo on the rack; at 6 he'll do anything while you wait.
