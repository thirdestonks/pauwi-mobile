What changed

  1. Pixel, not gradient. Hard-edged octagon and tiles with a black outline and a darker lip, like the rest of the pixel UI. Press = it sinks one pixel.
  2. The verb follows the weapon: SWING (bat, pipe), CHOP (itak), PUNCH (fists), SHOOT (9mm). Under it: the damage at the target's row, and the swings left as pips (rounds for the gun).
  3. Bigger, readable tiles: 16 px with a 9 px icon at the game's own scale, one colour per action (Guard teal, Item green, Swap amber, Run red), counts as corner badges, Run's odds on its plate.
  4. Pick a target on the street: tap a Shambler and the bracket moves; the damage on the button changes with its row (FAR hits for less). Inside a house there is one, already picked.
  5. The belt sits on the bottom edge beside the fan, never over the zombie. The other tiles dim while it is open; ✕ or the same tile closes it.
  6. States you can see: enemy turn dims the whole fan; Guard stays lit while raised; a weapon at 2 swings or less turns the button red and shakes (CRACKING); Attack breathes softly on your turn.
  7. Long-press = explain. Hold any button for a one-line description, so the icons can stay icon-only.
  8. Left-handed mirrors the fan to the left thumb (a setting in the game).
