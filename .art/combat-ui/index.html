Combat: two ways to choose

Only Juan's choices change. The fight, the hands, the zombies and the numbers stay exactly as they are. Today: five same-size text buttons (Attack, Guard, Item, Swap, Run), and Item/Swap open a list. Both concepts make Attack the obvious move, put items and weapons one tap away as pictures, and drop the list menus.

A · Thumb fan

One big ATTACK under the right thumb, four small round actions fanned around it.

  • Attack is the big button and shows the weapon in hand and its damage.
  • Guard, Item, Swap and Run are small icons in a fan, all under one thumb.
  • Item and Swap pop out a belt of pictures (bandage +12, itak 3 dmg); one tap uses it.
  • Still a menu, but a game one; easy to learn.

B · Hands on

Tap the zombie to swing. Tap your hand to guard. The belt is your kit.

  • Attack = tap the Shambler (a bracket shows who and how much). On the street you pick who in the same tap.
  • Guard = tap the weapon hand: he raises it.
  • The bottom edge is Juan's kit belt: items to use, weapons to swap (the one in hand is lit), and Run with its odds.
  • Matches the map and the shelter (you touch things, not buttons). Needs one first-fight hint.
