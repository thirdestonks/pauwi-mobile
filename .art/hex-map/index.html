Tondo · Hex Map

A faint purple glow marks a shop you haven't searched yet (+2 moves). A faint orange glow marks a shelter you could move to.

Tap a neighbouring hex to walk. A fresh tile costs a move, and tiles you've cleared are free. Props next to Juan hint at what's there: a backpack is loot, a body is danger, a crackling radio is an event. Step in to find out.

Cleared hexes get a green outline. They're free to cross, so it's your safe path home, and along it Juan dashes two hexes per tap. Hover a neighbouring hex to see what a step costs.

Three shelters to find: Tenement 10 to the north-east, Barangay 43 Hall to the south-east, and Mang Mel's Bike Shop to the south-west. Botika, Kwik Mart, Water Refilling and Kainan fronts dot the streets, and stepping in front of one lets you scavenge it.

Rosa's Tapsihan (pink sign) and Mabuhay Mart (green sign) give +2 moves when searched. Some loot spots are abandoned cars: tap the car to try the trunk, but 15% of the time the alarm goes off and 1–2 Shamblers come for it (win, and the trunk is still yours). Inside, 3 things are rolled from that spot's loot table. Tap one to look at it, tap again (or Take it) to carry it out. You only get one. Reach a shelter's door to relocate there. It becomes home, and the old zone's trail resets.

You get 5 moves per cycle. "Head back to shelter" ends the cycle at any time and flips day and night. Cleared ground stays faintly remembered in the dark. Tutuban stays on the horizon with its distance in tiles.
