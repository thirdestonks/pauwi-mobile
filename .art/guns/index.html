Guns · pump shotgun & M4

Your painted frames, lined up and pixelated, played over the night street. Fire and reload with the buttons (or keys: Space / R for the shotgun, F / T for the M4; hold F for automatic). The kick, the screen shake, the flash of light and the Shambler's flinch are done in code; the gun pictures are yours.

Pump shotgun

Fire → kick → pump back (shell pops) → forward → ready. Reload pushes in one shell at a time.
SHAMBLER · MID
  • 6 in the tube. One blast (5 damage) drops a Shambler at MID.
  • Each shot costs time: the pump before the next one.
  • Reload is one shell at a time, so a half-empty tube is quick to top up.

M4

Tap to fire once, hold for automatic. Reload: magazine out → in → racked.
SHAMBLER · MID
  • 30 in a magazine. 2 damage a round: three rounds drop a Shambler.
  • Automatic eats ammo fast. Ammo will be the M4's real cost.
  • The reload is long (2.4 s): one Shambler turn, in the real fight.
